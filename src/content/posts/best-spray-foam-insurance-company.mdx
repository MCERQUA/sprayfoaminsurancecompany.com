The spray foam industry has a coverage problem. Most contractors are insured — but far fewer are actually covered for the claims that matter most. Off-ratio incidents, chemical exposure, remediation costs, and completed-operations disputes routinely get denied because standard GL policies weren't built for spray foam.

The best spray foam insurance company solves this problem before a claim arrives. Here's what that looks like in practice.

## 1. Off-Ratio Coverage Is Non-Negotiable

Off-ratio spray foam — where the A and B components mix at the wrong ratio — is the most frequent and costly claim in the industry. It can cause:

- Structural foam failure requiring full removal
- Off-gassing of volatile organic compounds into occupied spaces
- Remediation costs running into six figures on commercial projects
- Warranty disputes with building owners and GCs

The best spray foam insurance company builds off-ratio coverage into the GL policy — or explains exactly why it's there and what it covers. A provider who doesn't know what off-ratio is shouldn't be writing your policy.

## 2. Contractor Pollution Liability (CPL) Coverage

MDI isocyanate, polyols, amine catalysts, and blowing agents are classified as pollutants under standard GL forms. That means the chemicals you work with every day are excluded from your liability coverage unless you have a contractor pollution liability endorsement or a dedicated CPL policy.

The best spray foam insurer includes CPL or clearly offers it as part of the program — not as an afterthought someone mentions when you ask.

## 3. Correct Workers' Comp Class Codes

Spray foam applicators are not painters. They're not generic construction workers. They work with hazardous chemicals, respiratory equipment, and specialized machinery. The correct workers' comp class codes for spray foam work reflect that risk profile.

Using the wrong class code can:
- Mean you're paying for coverage that doesn't match your crew's actual work
- Create audit exposure if the carrier recodes your workforce during an audit
- Lead to claim disputes about whether an injury is covered under the assigned code

The best spray foam insurance company knows the right codes and uses them.

## 4. Same-Day Certificates

Spray foam contractors deal with a constant stream of certificate requests from GCs, property owners, and facility managers. The best spray foam insurance company issues same-day certificates with:

- Correct additional insured endorsements
- Project-specific language when required
- Fast turnaround — hours, not days

This is a basic operational requirement for active spray foam contractors, not a premium feature.

## 5. A-Rated Carrier Partners

An insurance policy is only as good as the carrier behind it. A-rated carriers (per A.M. Best) have the financial strength to pay claims — even large remediation or completed-operations claims — without dispute or delay.

The best spray foam insurer places coverage with A-rated admitted carriers where possible, and A-rated surplus-lines markets for harder-to-place risks.

## 6. Specialty Market Access

Not every spray foam account is straightforward. Contractors with prior claims, larger commercial operations, or work in high-risk applications (roofing, commercial HVAC, industrial) may need specialty markets. The best spray foam insurance company has relationships with those markets and can place programs other brokers can't.

## 7. No Surprises at Claim Time

The defining feature of the best spray foam insurance company is what happens when a claim arrives. Your insurer should already have:

- Reviewed your policy for off-ratio and pollution exclusions before binding
- Confirmed completed-operations language and limits are appropriate
- Made sure your certificate correctly reflects the coverage in place

Claims should be a relief, not a revelation about what your policy actually says.
